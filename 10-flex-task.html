IIT Hyderabad student

who bagged Rs 2.5 crore package meets institute director

An IIT Hyderabad student secured a staggering Rs 2.5 crore package from a Netherlands-based firm, and a video of the student being congratulated by the institute's director went viral on social media. The video was shared on IIT Hyderabad's official Instagram handle, where the institute celebrated the milestone with the caption, "From hard work to a Rs 2.5 crore milestone — Edward Nathan Varghese meets Prof. B. S. Murty, sharing insights from his journey and celebrating success at IIT Hyderabad."
